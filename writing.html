Twenty-one pieces on Substack

Independent writing at sheersh13.substack.com — regulation, cost pressure, and AI, applied to retail and consumer packaged goods.

The Singularity Tax: What the AI Boom Costs in Jobs, Watts, Honest Accounting and Time

The AI boom's hidden costs across four fronts — job displacement (UK graduate vacancies down 45% YoY), energy consumption (Microsoft's emissions up 25%, Google's electricity use up 37% in 2025), accounting opacity in capex reporting, and grid-capacity constraints — arguing AI can verify sustainability data just as easily as it can generate convincing but unverified climate narratives at scale.

The Wars of the Roses: Why Sustainability Practice and Sustainability Reporting Never Learned to Speak to Each Other

The disconnect between the sustainability work companies actually do and the evidence they produce to prove it — two "houses" that speak different dialects and compete for resources, with reporting's regulatory deadlines crowding out practice's unenforced action, and why reporting without action creates exposure while action without evidence creates invisibility.

The Trust Threshold: How Consumers Are Actually Meeting the Agentic Supply Chain

The gap between AI shopping adoption and consumer trust — 42% of US consumers used AI tools to shop in the past month, but only 5% let agents complete purchases unsupervised, and 75% say paid placements in AI recommendations would undermine trust — arguing retailers' real opportunity in agentic commerce is building trustworthy systems, not just faster ones.

The Protein Premium: Is CPG's New Normal a Macronutrient?

Whether protein's rise across CPG — snacks, cereals, coffee, ice cream, beverages — is a durable structural shift driven by GLP-1 medication adoption, or just a copyable label claim, arguing lasting brand value only comes from genuine manufacturing/formulation advantages, not the word "protein" on the pack.

The Compounding Case: How Agentic AI Turns ESG Work Into Enterprise Value

Why agentic AI's real value in ESG isn't automating individual tasks but building shared infrastructure across them — reporting, ratings questionnaires, carbon accounting, KPI tracking — so each additional workflow gets cheaper, with efficiency and risk-avoidance compounding fastest and cost-of-capital gains arriving slower.

Beyond the Reporting Chapter: Mapping the Agentic-Feasible Gaps in ESG Operations

Where agentic AI can realistically help ESG operations beyond regulatory reporting — ESG-rating questionnaires, carbon-credit verification, covenant/compensation KPI tracking, supplier certifications, grievance triage — and why materiality and assurance stay institutional, human questions no matter how capable the models get.

Can Agentic AI Close CPG's ESG Reporting Gap? Same Data, Six Rulebooks

Why CPG companies' ESG challenge isn't data collection but reconciling the same supply-chain data across six reporting frameworks — and why agentic AI should automate the mechanical work around ESG judgment (data cleaning, unit conversion, cross-framework translation) while materiality, assurance, and sign-off stay human.

The Judgment Trade: When Agentic AI Makes Retail Easier; and Riskier

How autonomous AI is delivering real value in retail and logistics while also failing at judgment calls — and why the difference comes down to whether verification is built into the deployment from the start.
