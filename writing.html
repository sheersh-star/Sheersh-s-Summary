Sustainability, ESG & Agentic AI

Independent writing at sheersh13.substack.com; sustainability, ESG, and AI, applied to retail and consumer packaged goods.

Sustainability & ESG

Where most of the writing lives; ESG reporting, agentic AI applied to sustainability work, carbon and circularity, and the regulation reshaping all three. The throughline: a report is the receipt for work that actually happened, not a substitute for it.

Burhs, Not Heroes: Why Sustainability Needs Agentic AI, and How to Build It

On why annual sustainability reporting cycles fail against continuous environmental challenges; proposing agents deployed at every data source (energy meters, procurement, finance, suppliers) as a standing "garrison," modelled on King Alfred's fortified towns, that shifts teams from paperwork to actual decarbonization work.

Aut Caesar, Aut Nihil: Agentic AI Can Help Save the Environment, but Only If We Bury the Old World Order Properly

On why agentic AI accelerating emissions visibility and data-processing speed isn't enough on its own; detection without paired decision-making authority and budget just produces beautiful maps nobody acts on.

In a Fit of Absence of Mind: How Data Quietly Conquered the Skills Map

On how data literacy quietly became a non-negotiable professional skill, expanding as incrementally as the British Empire once did; using the spreadsheet revolution and AI advancement as case studies for why understanding data's origin, verifying its integrity, and knowing its limitations is now core to most careers.

Pipeline Before Paper: What the Anarchy Teaches Sustainability About Building First and Reporting Second

On why sustainability should build the opportunity pipeline (moving projects from idea to operation) before building reporting infrastructure; using England's 12th-century civil war as an analogy for why practice must precede data, which must precede reporting, not the reverse.

The Attention Standard: Why the World's Scarcest Currency Must Be Spent on Sustainability

On attention as humanity's scarcest, most exploited resource; arguing it should be redirected toward sustainability, a measurable and verifiable domain, with historical parallels to Byzantine infrastructure maintenance and a case for agentic AI reshaping how societies allocate cognitive focus.

Receipts, Not Reports: How Agentic AI Can End Sustainability's Data Famine and Get Practice Moving

On why sustainability's weakest link is unreliable underlying data, not poor reporting; agentic AI, properly built with verification safeguards, can improve data collection and streamline disclosure at once, as long as every emissions figure carries its reliability tier and source trail rather than a bare estimate.

The Seat Is Dead, Long Live the Invoice: How Goal-as-a-Service Rewrites Sustainability Reporting

On how Software-as-a-Service's per-seat pricing is being displaced by "Goal-as-a-Service" in sustainability reporting; agentic AI shifts the actual work from human operators to agents while humans move into approval roles, changing pricing from seats to measurable outputs and requiring clear data definitions and lineage before deployment.

The Singularity Tax: What the AI Boom Costs in Jobs, Watts, Honest Accounting and Time

The AI boom's hidden costs across four fronts; job displacement (UK graduate vacancies down 45% YoY), energy consumption (Microsoft's emissions up 25%, Google's electricity use up 37% in 2025), accounting opacity in capex reporting, and grid-capacity constraints; arguing AI can verify sustainability data just as easily as it can generate convincing but unverified climate narratives at scale.

The Wars of the Roses: Why Sustainability Practice and Sustainability Reporting Never Learned to Speak to Each Other

The disconnect between the sustainability work companies actually do and the evidence they produce to prove it; two "houses" that speak different dialects and compete for resources, with reporting's regulatory deadlines crowding out practice's unenforced action, and why reporting without action creates exposure while action without evidence creates invisibility.

The Compounding Case: How Agentic AI Turns ESG Work Into Enterprise Value

Why agentic AI's real value in ESG isn't automating individual tasks but building shared infrastructure across them; reporting, ratings questionnaires, carbon accounting, KPI tracking; so each additional workflow gets cheaper, with efficiency and risk-avoidance compounding fastest and cost-of-capital gains arriving slower.

Beyond the Reporting Chapter: Mapping the Agentic-Feasible Gaps in ESG Operations

Where agentic AI can realistically help ESG operations beyond regulatory reporting; ESG-rating questionnaires, carbon-credit verification, covenant/compensation KPI tracking, supplier certifications, grievance triage; and why materiality and assurance stay institutional, human questions no matter how capable the models get.

Can Agentic AI Close CPG's ESG Reporting Gap? Same Data, Six Rulebooks

Why CPG companies' ESG challenge isn't data collection but reconciling the same supply-chain data across six reporting frameworks; and why agentic AI should automate the mechanical work around ESG judgment (data cleaning, unit conversion, cross-framework translation) while materiality, assurance, and sign-off stay human.

From Plantation to P&L: How ESG Regulation Is Repricing Risk Across the CPG Shelf

ESG regulation reshaping ingredient, packaging, and sourcing risk for consumer packaged goods, using palm-oil traceability as the example.

Melting Margins. Rising Costs. No Place to Hide.

Cost pressures converging on the frozen dessert / ice cream industry; promotions, packaging, refrigeration mandates, marketing restrictions; and where future growth markets sit.

No Human Invented the Hexagon

What natural systems; fairy circles, beehives, hermit crab vacancy chains, weaver birds; suggest about disciplined resource use, applied to retail and real estate.

Every Product Has Two Price Tags. We Only Look at One.

Why sustainability data sits with the ESG team while decisions sit with procurement, manufacturing, and commercial; and what it would take for ESG reporting to become operational instead of retrospective.

The Third Price Tag

The environmental footprint of the AI systems built to monitor corporate sustainability; and why ESG monitoring tools need the same third-party validation discipline as the things they monitor.

FP&A Already Knows How to Forecast Uncertainty. ESG Hasn't Asked It To.

Why financial planning teams already have the forecasting discipline; scenarios, confidence intervals, quarterly reviews; that ESG reporting lacks, and what it would take to treat carbon like any other uncertain business variable.

Your AI Footprint Is a Liability, Until You Learn to Use It as an Advantage

Why AI tool spend creates unreported Scope 3 emissions liabilities, and why measuring it voluntarily now beats scrambling once auditors and regulation catch up.

Companies Are Quietly Doing More ESG While Announcing Less of It

The split between collapsing public ESG announcements and continued internal operational spending; and why metrics tied to genuine business risk outperform generic disclosure templates.

The Regulation Landed Today. The Money Reached Yesterday.

How beauty-industry refill economics preceded the EU's packaging regulation rather than followed it; circularity as a financial advantage, not just a compliance cost.

The Retailer of 2030 Sells Nothing You Can See

Six compounding bets shaping retail's future; selling to AI agents, retail media, ultra-fast delivery, circular economy models, automated checkout, and genuine AI personalization.
