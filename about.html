About

Sheersh Kachhwaha

Why

Sheersh's starting point is renewable energy, not business. The pull came from watching where the world is actually heading: oil and gas carry a level of political controversy, pollution, and historical conflict over resources that he doesn't believe the next chapter of the energy story needs to repeat, while renewable energy is a far more believable growth story as the innovation gap keeps closing. An MSc in Renewable Energy Engineering from Kingston University and a BSc in Environmental Science from Fergusson College, Pune, both sit on that conviction rather than a conventional business track; and it still shapes how he reads a client problem. Sustainability isn't a department to consult once the real analysis is done; it's the lens the numbers get run through first.

How

That conviction only matters if it survives contact with a spreadsheet. At Genpact, he supports structured, hypothesis-led analysis; MECE frameworks, issue trees; on cross-industry client advisory engagements, the same rigour he developed academically now tested in a live, client-facing setting. Independently, it shows up as a comparative investment case across renewable and conventional energy systems (CapEx, OpEx, LCOE, discount rate, carbon footprint), a lifecycle model spanning wind and solar assets, and a procurement analysis that turned system-efficiency modelling into a concrete material-substitution recommendation. He's increasingly applying AI-augmented and agentic workflows to that process; sped up without losing rigour; and treats technical detail and executive-ready narrative as two versions of the same output, not separate skills.

Strategy

The strategy is to keep sitting at the intersection of three things converging whether or not companies are ready for them: sustainability reporting, retail/CPG decision-making, and agentic AI. At Genpact, that means organising delivery across Finance, Real Estate, and Blueprinting & Solution Identification workstreams, and presenting agentic, Goal-as-a-Service solutions to client stakeholders; work that has to be planned and sold as carefully as it's built. Independently, it means writing in public about where that intersection is actually heading (sheersh13.substack.com, now mostly ESG and sustainability reporting), and keeping the project portfolio quantified rather than aspirational; so a case for reallocating capital toward renewables, or automating ESG's mechanical work while keeping judgment human, rests on real numbers, not a trend he's chasing. He considers it never too late to go back to formal study again, and treats refining this thinking as a matter of course, not a one-time positioning exercise.

Outside of work, badminton sits alongside a deeper, ongoing interest in nutrition, diet, fitness and sport, one that traces back to watching his mother work as a nutritionist; it turns out to be directly useful in CPG and retail work, where understanding the product; and how a consumer actually experiences it once it's on the shelf; matters as much as understanding the client. He's also fluent in English and Hindi, and currently learning Spanish.
