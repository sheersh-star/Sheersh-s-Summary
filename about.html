About

Sheersh Kachhwaha

Sheersh Kachhwaha is a Retail Advisory Analyst at Genpact, where he supports structured, hypothesis-led analysis — MECE frameworks, issue trees — on cross-industry client advisory engagements, building in a live, client-facing setting the same rigour he developed academically.

His path there runs through renewable energy engineering rather than a conventional business track. An MSc in Renewable Energy Engineering from Kingston University (advanced modelling across solar, wind and hydrogen systems) followed a BSc in Environmental Science from Fergusson College, Pune, where his undergraduate thesis was a cost study of contemporary and renewable energy resources. That technical grounding shows up directly in his independent work: a comparative investment case across renewable and conventional energy systems, a lifecycle model spanning wind and solar assets, and a procurement analysis that turned system-efficiency modelling into a concrete material-substitution recommendation.

What connects the engineering and the advisory work is a preference for analysis that earns its place through curiosity and follow-through, not just correctness — technical detail and executive-ready narrative are treated as two versions of the same output, not separate skills. He's increasingly applying AI-augmented workflows to that process, sped up without losing rigour, and writes about the same instinct applied outward: his Substack traces how regulation, cost pressure, and AI are converging on retail and CPG decision-making.

His pull toward renewable energy came from watching where the world is actually heading: oil and gas carry a level of political controversy, pollution, and historical conflict over resources that he doesn't believe the next chapter of the energy story needs to repeat, while renewable energy is a far more believable growth story as the innovation gap keeps closing. That same instinct shapes how he treats his own development — he considers it never too late to go back to formal study again, and keeps refining his thinking as a matter of course. Outside of work, badminton sits alongside a deeper, ongoing interest in nutrition, diet, fitness and sport, one that traces back to watching his mother work as a nutritionist; it turns out to be directly useful in CPG and retail work, where understanding the product — and how a consumer actually experiences it once it's on the shelf — matters as much as understanding the client. He's also fluent in English and Hindi, and currently learning Spanish.
