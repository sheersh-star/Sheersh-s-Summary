Independent analysis, energy sector

Three projects in the energy sector — two from the Freelance Strategist (Sustainability) engagement, one from MSc coursework at Kingston University.

Investment Case: Renewable vs. Conventional Energy Systems

What it solved: a comparative investment case across renewable and conventional energy assets — CapEx, OpEx, LCOE, discount rate, carbon footprint, and community impact.

Outcome: quantified renewables as the structurally superior long-term investment — near-zero lifecycle maintenance cost offsets higher upfront CapEx, with a materially lower carbon footprint, supporting a risk-adjusted case for reallocating capital away from coal and petroleum.

The Fair Energy Project — Wind Farm Proposal, Mablethorpe

What it did: sited and modelled a 5-turbine (Vestas V90, 2.0 MW each) wind farm at Mablethorpe beach, Lincolnshire — Weibull wind resource estimation, capacity factor and annual yield, and a full CAPEX/OPEX/LCOE financial case, solo, for MSc coursework (module ME7753, Kingston University).

Outcome: a profit-positive case within 6 years (5.5-year payback, 43.3% capacity factor, 37,962 MWh/year), sited to preserve the undisturbed Theddlethorpe dunes while creating 30–40 new local skilled/semi-skilled roles.

Procurement & Materials Cost Analysis

What it did: modelled system efficiency and energy yield for renewable assets using WindPro and SQL; benchmarked performance ratios to isolate cost and optimisation levers.

Outcome: delivered a validated material-substitution recommendation (carbon steel over tool steel), directly actionable for procurement and sourcing strategy.
