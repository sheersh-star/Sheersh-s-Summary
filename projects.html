ESG Meets CPG

What it did: built a regulatory intelligence console covering CPG ESG exposure across the EU, UK and US; a 19-row compliance calendar spanning 12 regulations (EUDR, CSRD/CSDDD, PPWR/UK pEPR/Plastic Tax, SEC Climate Rule, SB253/SB261, EmpCo/FTC Green Guides), ingredient/material exposure mapped to real alternative-sourcing approaches, real UK packaging datasets (NPWD, Producer Register, WRAP), a 14-entity retailer/delivery legal-risk ranking grounded in ownership and enforcement history, and a legislative & political forecast layer predicting which way each regulation is trending.

Outcome: every data row carries a source and a confidence label, never a fabricated precise figure; disputed facts were independently cross-checked rather than taken on faith (a contested regulatory deadline was corroborated across four independent law-firm sources before being treated as confirmed). All 12 tracked regulations now carry a researched trajectory. The one deliberately unbuilt piece is the company-level ESG rating layer: fully designed (five pillars, banded scoring, no invented precision) but not yet implemented, with the reputational risk of rating real companies named explicitly as something to design around, not discover later.

Frozen Dessert Market Console

What it did: built a Python data pipeline and synthesis engine into a self-updating, UK-first market-intelligence dashboard for the frozen dessert industry; regulatory exposure (UK FSA/WHO), production trends, regional inventory and equipment risk, and real-world outcome benchmarking, structured around a Problem/Action/Impact narrative.

Outcome: a zero-dependency, self-updating console; drop a new CSV into data/raw/ and every dependent panel updates automatically, no code changes or manual re-run required.

Procurement & Materials Cost Analysis

What it did: modelled system efficiency and energy yield for renewable assets using WindPro and SQL; benchmarked performance ratios to isolate cost and optimisation levers.

Outcome: delivered a validated material-substitution recommendation (carbon steel over tool steel), directly actionable for procurement and sourcing strategy.

Investment Case: Renewable vs. Conventional Energy Systems

What it solved: a comparative investment case across renewable and conventional energy assets; CapEx, OpEx, LCOE, discount rate, carbon footprint, and community impact.

Outcome: quantified renewables as the structurally superior long-term investment; near-zero lifecycle maintenance cost offsets higher upfront CapEx, with a materially lower carbon footprint, supporting a risk-adjusted case for reallocating capital away from coal and petroleum.

The Fair Energy Project; Wind Farm Proposal, Mablethorpe

What it did: sited and modelled a 5-turbine (Vestas V90, 2.0 MW each) wind farm at Mablethorpe beach, Lincolnshire; Weibull wind resource estimation, capacity factor and annual yield, and a full CAPEX/OPEX/LCOE financial case, solo, for MSc coursework (module ME7753, Kingston University).

Outcome: a profit-positive case within 6 years (5.5-year payback, 43.3% capacity factor, 37,962 MWh/year), sited to preserve the undisturbed Theddlethorpe dunes while creating 30-40 new local skilled/semi-skilled roles.
