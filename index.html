One Day at a Time

Changing the narrative around Sustainability, Retail, and Energy.

Sustainability is a systems problem, not a reporting problem: build the data pipeline first, let agents do the mechanical work, and keep people on judgment and sign-off. Sheersh Kachhwaha is a sustainability-trained, AI-educated Retail Advisory Analyst, building towards agentic AI strategy. He trained in renewable-energy engineering and environmental science before bringing that lens into retail advisory. At Genpact, he supports financial and investment decision-support; scenario modelling, sensitivity analysis, and return-on-investment analysis; for client engagements increasingly built around sustainability reporting and agentic AI. He writes independently on Substack, now mostly about ESG, sustainability reporting, and where AI is converging on both. Long-term aim: doctoral research on agentic AI and sustainability.
